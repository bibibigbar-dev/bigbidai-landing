Privacy Policy

Last updated: Aug 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Bigbid AI (“Bigbid AI,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, discloses, and protects information when you use our website, applications, AI-powered product listing tools, and related services (collectively, the “Service”).

By using the Service, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

Account Information

When you create or manage an account, we may collect information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Account identifiers
  • Company, seller, reseller, or business information you choose to provide
  • Authentication information managed by our authentication service provider

Passwords may be handled and stored by our authentication provider rather than directly by Bigbid AI.

Product and User Content

When you use Bigbid AI, you may upload or create information including:

  • Product photos and images
  • Product names and descriptions
  • Lot or listing information
  • Product condition information
  • SKU, item number, or other product identifiers
  • Retail price or MSRP information
  • Auction, resale, or bidding information
  • Other product metadata submitted through the Service

AI Processing Data

To provide AI-powered features, uploaded images and product information may be processed by artificial intelligence systems.

This processing may generate information such as:

  • Product identification
  • Product titles
  • Product descriptions
  • Product categories
  • Estimated retail prices
  • Suggested bidding or selling prices
  • Other listing-related information

AI-generated information may be stored with your account or product records as necessary to provide the Service.

Usage Information

We may collect information about how you interact with the Service, including:

  • Features you use
  • Items or listings you create
  • AI analysis requests
  • Export activity
  • Subscription usage
  • Pages or screens viewed
  • Dates and times of activity

Billing Information

If you purchase a subscription or other paid service, payment information is processed by third-party payment processors such as Stripe.

We may receive information such as:

  • Subscription plan
  • Payment status
  • Billing date
  • Transaction identifiers
  • Purchase and subscription history

Bigbid AI does not directly store full payment card numbers.

Device and Log Information

We may automatically collect limited technical information such as:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Date and time of access
  • Error logs
  • Security and diagnostic information

We use this information to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the Service.

  • Create, authenticate, and manage user accounts
